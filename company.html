About Our Company

Built on Fiber Optics,
Driven by Trust

We are a specialized FTTA & fiber optic connectivity supplier from Shenzhen, China — combining deep industry experience with dependable factory partnerships to serve telecom and network builders worldwide.

Our Story

Taisun Optical was founded by professionals who have spent over 12 years working across the fiber optic industry — from patch cords and transceivers, to optical modules and PON (ONU/OLT) equipment. We know this industry from end to end: what engineers demand, what installers struggle with, and what procurement teams truly care about.

Today we focus on what we do best — FTTA & outdoor fiber connectivity: waterproof connectors, ruggedized cable assemblies and the accessories that keep fiber networks reliable in the field.

  • 12+ years of hands-on experience in optical communications
  • Specialized focus on FTTA, FTTx and outdoor connectivity
  • Close partnerships with ISO-certified manufacturing facilities
  • Customer-first service — one point of contact from inquiry to delivery

Our Quality Assurance Process

Every order follows a disciplined 4-step quality workflow.

Incoming Inspection

Raw materials and connectors are inspected against specification before entering production.

Assembly & Termination

Skilled technicians assemble assemblies with precision; each unit is visually checked.

100% Optical Testing

Insertion loss, return loss, waterproof rating and insertion/withdrawal cycles are tested on every piece.

Packaging & Shipping

Secure, export-grade packaging with clear labeling; tested again before dispatch.

Supply Chain & Manufacturing

We operate a light-asset, partnership-driven model: rather than owning one rigid factory, we work with a carefully selected network of ISO-certified manufacturing partners in Shenzhen and Dongguan. This lets us match the right production line to every product type — and keeps our pricing honest.

For our customers this means:

  • Access to multiple certified production lines, not a single facility
  • Competitive pricing backed by real factory relationships
  • Scalable capacity from small samples to bulk container orders
  • Quality control supervised by our own team at every step
