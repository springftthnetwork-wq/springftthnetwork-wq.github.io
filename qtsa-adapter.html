The problem it solves

When your switch is QSFP-only, but your link is not

A QTSA turns one QSFP cage into a single SFP/SFP+ slot. Use the optics you already stock, connect to a lower-speed peer, and upgrade the other side later — on your schedule.

01

QSFP-only platform

Your switch, server or router exposes only QSFP cages. Plug the adapter in — no new line card, no forklift upgrade.

02

Lower-speed peer

The far end runs at 10G or 25G. Drop one lane down instead of forcing a full 40G/100G deployment.

03

Protect your optics

Reuse existing SFP+, SFP28, AOC and DAC inventory. One adapter, many module options.
