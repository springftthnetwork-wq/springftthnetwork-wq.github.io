FTTA Deployment, Made Simple

Fiber-to-the-Antenna moves optical signals from the equipment room up to the antenna. Our waterproof connectors protect every outdoor mating point.

Typical FTTA architecture: indoor BBU/OLT → feeder cable → junction point → tower riser → waterproof FTTA connectors → RRU & antenna.
